# Landing page — Cap Entrepreneur

Scope: the single public landing page (frontend/src/pages/Landing.tsx and its sections). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: Moroccan and other foreign students in France preparing a change of status to "entrepreneur / profession libérale". Action: upload a PDF CV + one paragraph about the future project, pay, receive a business plan. Proof: residence-permit photo (illustrative, data masked), official dated sources, sample business plan outline. Constraints: never promise a permit; no invented testimonials or numbers; palette pinned (#606c38 #283618 #fefae0 #dda15e #bc6c25).

## Direction contract

seed: a2d123cb (roll key as printed; no separate seed reference was printed. The user chose the pick card "Le dossier de préfecture")

THESIS: The page is the applicant's own prefecture file, opened on the desk: a kraft folder whose numbered pieces get stamped as they are supplied. It refuses the SaaS split hero and the row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Forest-green blotter (#283618) as the desk; kraft folder board (#dda15e) with olive (#606c38) tab dividers; cream sheets (#fefae0) for every readable piece; rust (#bc6c25) reserved for stamps, the active piece and the primary action. Paperclips, folder tabs, numbered "Pièce n°" headers, perforated slip edges. Archivo (wide/condensed) for headings and stamps, Atkinson Hyperlegible for reading.

STORY: 1) this is your file, and it starts here; 2) three pieces: CV, projet, paiement; 3) what the file contains once complete (business plan outline); 4) the procedure, sourced (bordereau des démarches + questionnaire); 5) an example of the permit mention (illustrative); 6) price slip, FAQ index cards.

FORM: The page is one object, the open prefecture folder, seen from above on the desk; sections below are further sheets and bands of the same file material (cream sheets, kraft board, olive divider), never extra folders.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The open folder fills the screen: left flap carries the headline and the piece count; right flap holds the working stack — account, CV drop zone, project paragraph, pay button — the real controls, not a picture.

SIGNATURE: Supplying a piece slides it into the stack and a rust "REÇU" stamp presses onto it (scale, rotate, blur settling); the folder counter advances.

RISK: Kraft + cream + rust sits in the warm-paper cluster; the pinned palette earns it, but every surface must look like file material, never a beige template.
